# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in TIKI a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give and accept technical feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not share microscopy data without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
trolling, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to **peterhogg@fas.harvard.edu**.
Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible.
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for behavior deemed inappropriate.
